Storage and timers

Songs, schedules, notes and practice history sync privately to your account. This browser keeps a local copy for connection interruptions. Check the sync status before switching devices. Export a backup for an additional copy.

Practice counts up until you pause or finish. It continues beyond your daily target and while the tab is closed. Close the practice card to hide it without stopping the timer.

The ring shows today's total against the scheduled target, or the song's default practice duration if unscheduled. The target chimes once per running session per day. Keep the page open and device awake for audio.

YouTube lessons require internet access. If a video cannot be embedded, use Open on YouTube.
